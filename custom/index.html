Neon: The Speed of Lightning

Neon is a lightning-fast Duelist in VALORANT,
charging into battle with electrifying speed.
Her electric powers
and relentless pace make her a lethal force,
striking down enemies before they can react.

Relay Bolt (Q)

Throws a bouncing bolt that stuns
enemies.

High Gear (E)

Gives extreme speed and a slide;
slide resets on kills.

Fast Lane (C)

Creates two electric walls that
block vision for pushing sites.

Gameplay Role

As a Duelist, Neon is all about mobility and aggression. She is built to:
Rush into sites
Create chaos with movement and utility
Take fast fights and outmaneuver enemies
Neon’s kit focuses heavily on speed, making her one of the more dynamic and unpredictable Agents to play.
